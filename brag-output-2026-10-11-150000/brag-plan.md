# Connect — launch video plan

## Angle
A single Amrita campus hub that turns a crowded student schedule into a clear path: discover the right opportunity, detect conflicts before they happen, and keep the official proof.

## Audience
Amrita students who juggle events, research, travel and attendance; faculty coordinators who need reliable approvals and records.

## Tone and identity
Polished, institutional, warm: the app’s actual cream (#E5DED2), burgundy (#490B1A), navy (#142841) and blue-grey (#798DA6) palette.

## Storyboard — 20 seconds
1. 0–7.1s — Events: search the real product vocabulary for robotics and surface relevant research/workshop opportunities.
2. 7.1–11.5s — Calendar: show the academic calendar and the interval-clash warning on Oct 18.
3. 11.5–17.7s — OD records: move from verified OD card to official, printable certificate.
4. 17.7–20s — Outro: Connect’s promise and campus identity.
